## 1. Executive Technical Findings (Evidence-Based)

### 1.1 What Already Exists & Works Well
1. **Purchase Invoices, Partial Receipts & Supplier Payables:**
   - `Supplier`, `SupplierInvoice`, `SupplierInvoiceLine`, `SupplierPayment`, `SupplierReceipt` (`apps/api/app/modules/finance/models.py`).
   - `POST /api/v1/finance/supplier-invoices`, `POST .../receive` (partial & full GRN, updates `StockLedgerEntry` and posts `Dr 1210 / Cr 2100`), `POST .../payments`, `POST .../payment-requests/full-balance` (`apps/api/app/api/v1/endpoints/finance.py`).
   - Authoritative calculations in `apps/api/app/modules/finance/balances.py` (`supplier_invoice_financials`, `supplier_totals`).
   - UI in `apps/web/src/features/finance/SupplierPurchasingView.tsx`.
2. **Sales Orders, Deliveries, Returns & Collections Separation:**
   - `CustomerOrder`, `CustomerOrderLine`, `CustomerDelivery`, `CustomerDeliveryLine`, `CustomerDeliveryReturn`, `CustomerDeliveryReturnLine`, `CustomerPayment` (`apps/api/app/modules/customers/models.py`).
   - Authoritative calculations in `balances.py` (`customer_order_financials`, `document_net_sales_value`, `customer_totals`).
   - Delivery deducts `finished_goods` stock (`factory_brand` or `private_label:{customer_id}`) and posts `Dr 1100 / Cr 4100` + `Dr 5100 / Cr 1230`. Returns quarantine stock (`return_pending_inspection:{cid}`) and inspect split restock/scrap (`apps/api/app/api/v1/endpoints/customers.py`).
   - Sales & profitability analytics exist in `apps/api/app/modules/reports/analytics.py` (`sales_analysis`, `model_profitability`, `aging`, `overview`, `payroll_cost`, `losses`, `stock_health`).
3. **Treasury, Custody, Cash Ledger & Double-Entry GL Core:**
   - `CashAccount`, `CashDocument`, `CashLedgerEntry`, `CustodySettlement` + append-only PostgreSQL trigger `trg_financial_ledger_append_only` (`0017`).
   - `Account`, `JournalEntry`, `JournalEntryLine` (`0016`) + `post_journal_entry` & `get_weighted_average_cost` in `ledger_service.py` with 15 seeded accounts (`1010`–`5400`).
   - Central Treasury Maker-Checker + `POST_ON_CREATION_ROLES = {"finance_manager"}` (`finance/service.py`).
4. **Payroll & Attendance Foundation:**
   - `Employee` (`monthly` / `daily` pay types), `EmployeeAttendance`, `PayPeriod`, `PayPeriodEvent`, `PayrollRun`, `PayrollRunItem` (`production/models.py`, `endpoints/payroll.py`), Excel export, and Treasury disbursement.

### 1.2 Critical Gaps, Bugs, Placeholders & Flaws Discovered
1. **Orphaned & Broken Legacy Screen (`PaymentsCollectionsView.tsx`):**
   - `apps/web/src/features/finance/PaymentsCollectionsView.tsx` is wired to route `finance-payments` in `App.tsx` (line 911) and `ROUTE_PERMISSIONS`, **but does not appear in any sidebar navigation array**.
   - It checks `inv.status === 'paid'` / `'partially_paid'` (lines 146–151), which do **not** exist in `SupplierInvoice` (`'awaiting_receipt'`, `'partially_received'`, `'received'`, `'cancelled'`).
2. **Broken GL Account Statement Endpoint (`reports.py:1111-1112`):**
   - `GET /api/v1/reports/general-ledger/{account_code}` exists in `reports.py:1070`, computes running balance by account nature, **but lines 1111–1112 read `je.reference_type` and `je.reference_id`**, whereas `JournalEntry` defines `source_doc_type` and `source_doc_id` (`finance/models.py:177-178`). Calling it on non-empty data raises `AttributeError`. Also not exposed in UI and lacks period/opening-balance support.
3. **Account `5200` Overloading & Double-Counting Risk:**
   - `5200` ("أجور ومصروفات تصنيع مباشرة") currently receives: ALL payroll cash disbursements (`finance/service.py:154`), WIP transit losses (`production/service.py:660`, `extra_issue.py:370`), WIP closing scrap/variance (`production.py:2033`), stocktake shortages AND surpluses (`stocktake_service.py:653,666`), and scrapped customer returns (`customers.py:1184`).
   - Meanwhile, `ProductionOrder.actual_labor_cost` and `actual_overhead_cost` exist in DB (`0016`) but are always `0.00`. Adding labor costing without separating payroll clearing (`2200` / wage absorption) would double-count wages in P&L.
4. **Balance Sheet Accounting Foundations Missing (`reports.py:969`):**
   - `GET /api/v1/reports/balance-sheet` exists in backend (hidden from UI per `KNOWN_LIMITATIONS_AR.md`), but: (a) has no `as_of` date filter, (b) hardcodes only 9 account codes (`1010..3000`), (c) has no Opening Balances workflow (`3000` is `0.00`; opening cash posts to `4200` revenue; opening stocktake posts to `5200`), (d) `1030` Bank has no `CashAccount` type (`ck_cash_accounts_type` only allows `treasury`, `petty_cash`), (e) no Fixed Assets/Depreciation, no Accrued Payroll, no pre-0016 GL history.
5. **Keyword-Based Expense Classification (`finance/service.py:156`):**
   - `cash_expense` picks GL account `5300` vs `5400` by checking if `"صيانة"`, `"تشغيل"`, or `"إنتاج"` appears in `description`.
6. **Production Performance Endpoint Returns Placeholder Constants (`production.py:1456-1525`):**
   - `GET /api/v1/production/performance/summary` ignores actual timestamps, appends static `STANDARD_STAGE_DURATIONS_MINUTES` for completed stages, and hardcodes `overall_cycle_avg_hours=8.5` and `delayed_orders=[]`.
   - `ProductionStageEvent` (`production/models.py:447`) stores only ONE row per `(order_id, stage_name)` and **overwrites** `timestamp` on every transition (`not_started` → `in_progress` → `completed`). While `ProductionStageHistory` (`0002`) logs transitions, neither table has `started_at`, `completed_at`, `expected_duration`, `elapsed_minutes`, or delay reason fields, and `ProductionOrder` has no `started_at` / `completed_at` / `expected_completion_at`.
7. **External Manufacturing Only Partially Modeled:**
   - `ProductionOrderItem.product_type` supports `'factory_brand'` vs `'private_label'` (`customer_id`), and finished goods are segregated in `StockLedgerEntry.bucket_reference = 'private_label:{customer_id}'`.
   - However, there is **zero support** for: customer-owned raw material receipts/custody/reconciliation, accessories ownership flags (`customer` vs `factory`), waste allowance %, labor costing (time/piece), fixed-price external manufacturing billing/revenue (`4200` separation), or internal vs. external P&L reporting.
8. **Missing Finance UI Views:**
   - No **Purchased Items** flat history view or endpoint.
   - No **Sold Items** line-level view or endpoint.
   - No **Balance Sheet**, **Trial Balance**, **GL Ledger**, **Employee Statement**, **Treasury Statement**, or **Custody Statement** UI screens.
   - `OperationsBoard.tsx:333` hardcodes `realDeliveries = []`.
   - `MachineryView.tsx` is unfinished and hidden.
